I'm a PhD candidate studying under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Ingmar Weber, at the Chair of Societal Computing, University of Saarland. My work focuses on use of computer vision, to study human mobility from satellite imagery.

Research

I develop machine learning methods for analysing satellite imagery, with applications in mobility analysis, human displacement, and public health. My recent work explores weakly supervised learning for parking lot occupancy estimation.
